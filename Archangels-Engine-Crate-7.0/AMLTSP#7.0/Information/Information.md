# Thanks for downloading Archangel Motors Long Term Support Pack #7.0!



# Information:

LTSPs, aka Long Term Support Packages, aim to provide additional content and improvements to previously released CEPs, also known as Crate Engine Packs.

LTSP#7 Brings the Wankel engines and some minor changes.
To find out what changed exactly, you may read throught the following changelog:

## **LTSP7.0**

**Wankel Engines**: Implemented wankel engines. Same automated calculations, although a bit less refined than reciprocating engines either due to lack of relevant literature to base math on or Piranha limitations.

**Turbulence and Flame Speed Adjustments**: Tweaked the flame speed to get even more realistic PVs. Slightly changed the default turbulence values to make them active by default.

**Minor intake and exhaust tweaks**: Slight reduction in flows to prevent critical scenarios. New universal exhaust collector sizing formula.

**Obsolete Parts Removal**: Removed Qmax intakes and exhausts. Removed N-in-1 exhaust collectors.

**Drivetrain Tweaks**: Readjusted default drivetrain parameters and calculations to reduce the overall default jerkiness and weakness of drivetrains. Manual tweaks are still advised for best results.
